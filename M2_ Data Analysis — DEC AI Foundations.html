The Problem: Data Overload at DEC Infra

DEC Infra manages 12 concurrent projects generating 242+ budget line items, hundreds of vendor invoices, and weekly schedule updates. A finance manager doing this manually faces:

📋 Manual Process

  • Open 12 separate Excel sheets
  • Cross-reference budget vs actual per item
  • Calculate variance for 242 rows
  • Identify which items are over budget
  • Check contingency across all projects
  • Write an executive summary
  • Create charts and dashboards

⏱ Time: 4-6 hours per weekly review

🤖 With AI Analysis

  • Upload combined CSV to ChatGPT/Claude
  • Paste one analysis prompt
  • AI scans ALL 242 rows simultaneously
  • AI flags every overrun, risk, anomaly
  • AI generates executive summary
  • AI provides recommendations & forecasts
  • Ask follow-up for deeper analysis

📂 Part 4: Case Studies — AI Catches What Humans Miss

CASE STUDY CS-1: The ₹2.9L RCC Overrun Hidden in 242 Rows

▼

📍 Situation

Nizamabad Water Plant monthly report shows "on track" — overall 74.1% spent vs 75% planned. Looks fine at project level. But nobody drills into the 23 individual line items.

🤖 What AI Found

Portfolio Risk Scan prompt flagged RCC Columns & Beams at 114.9% spent — ₹2.9L over budget, the single largest overrun in the entire 242-row dataset. The overrun was masked because earthwork and plastering were under budget, averaging out the total. AI also found contingency for this item was already 73% burned with 30% of work remaining.

💰 Impact

Manual discovery: at project end (too late). AI discovery: at 74% completion (time to act). PM froze scope additions, renegotiated remaining rebar quantities. Saved ₹1.8L vs doing nothing. Time to find it manually: 3-4 hours scanning 242 rows. Time with AI: 90 seconds.

CASE STUDY CS-2: ₹4.8L Saved by Comparing Rebar Rates

▼

📍 Situation

Each of DEC Infra's 12 projects negotiates rebar independently. Nobody compares rates across sites because data lives in separate spreadsheets managed by different PMs.

🤖 What AI Found

Vendor Rate Deep-Dive revealed: IT Park pays ₹51/kg, Medical College pays ₹65/kg — a 27.5% gap for identical-grade TMT steel. SWC and Kamareddy also overpaying at ₹63/kg. AI calculated: standardizing at ₹54/kg (volume discount across portfolio) would save ₹4.8-6.2L annually.

🎯 Action

DEC Infra centralized rebar procurement. Single vendor negotiation across all sites. Result: ₹54/kg rate locked for 12 months. Annual savings: ₹8-12L. This one AI prompt paid for the entire training program.

CASE STUDY CS-3: Kamareddy Contingency — 6 Weeks from Disaster

▼

📍 Situation

Kamareddy Auditorium has 4 overrun items and its HVAC contingency is 91% burned. Medical College piling contingency is 88% burned. Meanwhile, IT Park and JNTU have healthy untouched contingency reserves.

🤖 What AI Found

Contingency Health Check revealed: At current burn rate, Kamareddy would exhaust ALL contingency in 6 weeks. Medical College in 8 weeks. But IT Park had ₹0.4L barely touched, JNTU had ₹0.4L untouched — both projects under budget and won't need it.

💰 Impact

CFO approved reallocating ₹5L from healthy projects to stressed projects. Without this: Kamareddy would pause work (₹3-5L in idle labor costs) or request emergency board approval (3-week delay). AI analysis done in 2 minutes. Manual: Would require a full-day cross-portfolio review that nobody had time for.

CASE STUDY CS-4: ₹9.87L Invoice Stuck for 45 Days

▼

📍 Situation

JNTU Campus has a ₹9.87L pending invoice for piling work — the largest pending invoice in the entire portfolio. The vendor is threatening to stop work on another DEC Infra project.

🤖 What AI Found

Invoice Gap Analysis revealed: 10 invoices totaling ₹54L are pending across the portfolio. The JNTU ₹9.87L invoice was approved but stuck in payment processing. Meanwhile, 3 invoices totaling ₹8.5L were linked to over-budget items — flagged for investigation before payment.

🎯 Action

Clear the 7 undisputed invoices (₹45.5L) immediately. Investigate 3 disputed invoices (₹8.5L) before payment. JNTU piling invoice cleared same day — vendor relationship preserved. One prompt prevented a vendor walkout that would have delayed JNTU by 3 weeks (₹8-10L impact).

CAUTIONARY CS-5: When AI Got the Recommendation Wrong

▼

📍 What Went Wrong

AI recommended dropping Srinivasa Constructions (₹63/kg rebar at Kamareddy) in favor of a cheaper vendor. Srinivasa also appears at Gajwel at ₹55/kg — so they can clearly supply at lower rates.

💥 What AI Didn't Know

Srinivasa has a 12-year relationship with DEC Infra. They provide 24-hour emergency deliveries, 90-day credit terms, and have never missed a deadline. The ₹63/kg rate at Kamareddy included trucking to a remote location (2 hours from Hyderabad). The cheaper vendor would charge ₹12/kg extra for delivery — making them MORE expensive. AI analyzed data, not relationships.

✅ Lesson

AI analyzes what's IN the data. It doesn't know what's NOT in the data. Vendor relationships, delivery reliability, credit terms, site accessibility — these don't appear in spreadsheets. Always layer human judgment on AI analysis. AI is the starting point, never the final answer.
