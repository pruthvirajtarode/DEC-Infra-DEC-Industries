PART 2: LIVE DEMONSTRATION (15 MINUTES)
================================================================================

STEP 1: OPEN AI TOOL (2 minutes)
- Open ChatGPT or Claude in web browser (projector view)
- Have dataset open in Excel alongside (split screen)

STEP 2: COPY & PASTE DATA (2 minutes)
- Select all data in Excel (Ctrl+A)
- Copy (Ctrl+C)
- Paste into ChatGPT prompt
- Show the data is now in AI tool

STEP 3: RUN FIRST PROMPT - PORTFOLIO RISK SCAN (3 minutes)

Copy this exact prompt:

---BEGIN PROMPT---
Analyze this multi-project budget dataset from DEC Infra (150+ line items, 
10 projects). I'm pasting the complete CSV data below.

[PASTE YOUR DATA HERE]

Please provide:
1. RED FLAGS: List any line items exceeding 105% spent (over budget)
2. BUDGET HEALTH BY PROJECT: Which projects are healthiest? Which are at risk?
3. CONTINGENCY ANALYSIS: Which projects are burning contingency fastest?
4. FORECAST: If current spending continues, what will our final project costs be?
5. TOP 3 COST-SAVING OPPORTUNITIES: Where can we control costs immediately?

Format as executive summary (250 words max). Use actual project names and 
rupee amounts. Be specific and actionable.

---END PROMPT---

EXPECTED OUTPUT (60-90 seconds):
- AI identifies Medical College piling overage (109.4%)
- IISER foundation pressure (107%)
- NSDL Data Center power supply contingency burn (2.0L used)
- Portfolio forecast: ₹694.8L → likely ₹710-715L if trends continue
- Action items: Lock medical college pile scope, renegotiate finish rates at IISER

PAUSE HERE & ASK AUDIENCE:
"How long would this analysis take manually? 2-3 hours? AI did it in 90 seconds. 
And notice - it didn't just give numbers. It gave you specific project names and 
amounts to act on immediately. That's the power of AI + structured data."

STEP 5: COST SAVINGS PROMPT (3 minutes)

Copy this:

---BEGIN PROMPT---
Which cost categories have the most variance across projects? For example:
- Are MEP costs higher on some projects than others?
- Are finishes budgeted conservatively on some projects but aggressively on others?

Help me identify 3 line items where we're over-budgeting or could negotiate better 
rates. Show me the specific category, project, current amount, and savings potential.

---END PROMPT---

EXPECTED OUTPUT:
• FLOORING COSTS: NSDL (₹4200/sqm) vs Kollur Housing (₹180/sqm) = Specification 
  difference. Renegotiate marble supplier now = ₹2-3L savings
• LABOR RATES: Some projects use ₹500/unit for steel, others ₹800 = standardize 
  rates across portfolio = ₹8-12L savings
• MEP CONTINGENCY: Data Center at 3.5L, IT Park at 1.5L = lock equipment prices 
  before Q4 rate increases = ₹3-5L savings

TOTAL: ₹13-20L in potential savings (1.9-2.9% of portfolio) identified in 3 prompts.

================================================================================
PART 3: HANDS-ON EXERCISE (20 MINUTES)
================================================================================

DIVIDE PARTICIPANTS INTO 5 GROUPS:
- Group 1: Finance/PM (Risk Analysis)
- Group 2: Finance (Variance Analysis)
- Group 3: Cost Control (Benchmarking)
- Group 4: Project Managers (Phase Analysis)
- Group 5: Site Supervisors (Labor & Timeline Risk)

ASSIGN EACH GROUP A TASK (provide pre-written prompts):

---GROUP 1: RISK ANALYSIS---
Task: Identify all line items at risk of overrun

Prompt:
"Using the data, find all line items where:
1. Spent % is above 90%
2. Remaining budget is less than 1 lac
3. Contingency has been used

What's the combined overrun risk? If all of these slip by 10%, how much will 
it cost? Recommend 3 mitigation actions."

Expected Time: 4 minutes
Share Results: 2 minutes

---GROUP 2: VARIANCE ANALYSIS---
Task: Compare spending patterns by project phase

Prompt:
"Analyze civil works vs MEP vs finishes across all projects:
1. What's the average % spent in each phase?
2. Why is civil at 70% while finishes are at 15%?
3. Which phase has most variance (cost overruns)?
4. If finishes phase gets compressed (timeline pressure), what happens to costs?

Give forecast: if we compress finishes by 2 months, how much extra will labor cost?"

Expected Time: 4 minutes
Share Results: 2 minutes

---GROUP 3: BENCHMARKING---
Task: Find cost optimization opportunities

Prompt:
"Line items across projects include different rates (₹/unit). For example:
- Concrete rates vary
- Labor rates vary
- Material rates vary

Identify 5 specific line items where we're paying premium rates. What should 
we pay based on lowest price seen? How much can we save if we standardize rates?

Show: Item | Current Rate | Benchmark Rate | Projects Affected | Savings Potential"

Expected Time: 4 minutes
Share Results: 2 minutes

---GROUP 4: PHASE ANALYSIS---
Task: Forecast project completion costs & timeline

Prompt:
"For IISER Library and Medical College (our 2 largest projects):
1. What's the completion target % for each phase?
2. How much budget remains?
3. At current burn rate, when will each project reach completion?
4. Which phase is at risk of budget overrun?

Forecast final project cost vs original budget. Which project will exceed budget?"

Expected Time: 4 minutes
Share Results: 2 minutes

---GROUP 5: LABOR & TIMELINE---
Task: Identify labor cost risks

Prompt:
"Which phases are at 80%+ completion but still have high budget remaining? 
This suggests either:
- Efficient work (good)
- Compressed timeline ahead (costly)
- Labor rate inflation coming

For each such item, what's the risk? How do we manage labor costs if timelines 
compress and we need to fast-track work?"

Expected Time: 4 minutes
Share Results: 2 minutes

RECORDING RESULTS:
- Have 1 person from each group present (90 seconds each)
- Record findings on flip chart or shared screen
- Highlight: How quickly AI identified complex patterns

PART 5: OPTIONAL DEEP-DIVE PROMPTS (FOR ADVANCED PARTICIPANTS)
================================================================================

These are "bonus" prompts you can run if time allows or share for participants 
to try at their desk:

---PROMPT: CONTINGENCY BURN RATE FORECAST---
"Using contingency allocated and contingency used data, forecast:
1. How many months until contingency is fully consumed (at current burn rate)?
2. Which projects will run out of contingency first?
3. What happens if we have a major delay (10% slip on all projects)?
4. Should we reallocate contingency from low-risk to high-risk projects?

Give me a month-by-month forecast."

---PROMPT: VENDOR PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS---
"The dataset includes vendor names. Analyze which vendors are delivering on budget:
1. Which vendors have cost overruns?
2. Which vendors are under budget (efficient)?
3. For vendors with overruns, is it project-specific or across all projects?
4. Should we renegotiate or switch vendors?

Recommend top 3 vendors to use for future projects."

---PROMPT: LABOR PRODUCTIVITY ANALYSIS---
"Using unit rates and quantities, estimate labor costs:
1. Which line items have highest labor cost per unit?
2. Are these justified (complex work) or inflated?
3. On which projects is labor most efficient?
4. Can we apply efficient projects' practices to inefficient ones?

Show: Project | Line Item | Labor Cost Per Unit | Benchmark | Variance"

---PROMPT: TIMELINE COMPRESSION RISK---
"Identify phases that are <30% spent but scheduled to complete within 3 months:
1. Which projects have this risk?
2. If we compress these phases, labor costs increase by 20-30%. How much?
3. Which phases should we de-scope to avoid compression?
4. Alternative: start finishes phase before structure is 100% done?

Give me a risk-mitigation timeline."
