# DEC Company Overview

## Organization Structure
DEC is a diversified corporate group consisting of three main divisions:
1. **DEC Infra**: Construction and infrastructure organization.
2. **DEC Industries**: Manufacturing and operations.
3. **DEC Agro**: Agriculture and dairy ecosystem.

## Business Context
The group handles large scale workflows including:
- Project planning & milestones
- Vendor management and procurement
- Heavy machinery and materials tracking
- Site operations and manpower/attendance
- Financial ledgers, invoices, and purchase orders

## Training Mandate
The "DEC AI Foundations" platform is designed to upskill employees across all departments (Accounts, HR, Site Ops, Procurement, etc.) to securely and efficiently integrate AI into their specific daily tasks.

> **SYNTHETIC DATA POLICY**
> All data used in this training environment is strictly synthetic. No actual company financial results, personal employee data, or confidential project values are included.
