# DEC Agro Context

## Overview
DEC Agro is the agricultural technology and agribusiness division of the DEC Group, committed to sustainable farming and food security.

## Core Operations
- **Smart Farming**: Utilizing IoT sensors, drones, and AI for precision agriculture.
- **Agri-Logistics**: Operating cold storage chains and efficient transportation networks for perishable goods.
- **Food Processing**: Transforming raw agricultural output into value-added consumer products.

## Key Terminology
- **Yield per Hectare**: A standard measurement for agricultural productivity.
- **Cold Chain**: A temperature-controlled supply chain for perishables.
- **Agronomy**: The science of soil management and crop production.

## Standard Operating Procedures
- Soil health data must be analyzed before recommending fertilizer applications.
- Cold storage facilities must log temperature variations every hour.
- Traceability systems must track produce from farm gate to retail shelves.
