# DEC Industries Context

## Overview
DEC Industries is the manufacturing and heavy engineering arm of the DEC Group, focused on producing high-grade materials and machinery.

## Core Operations
- **Steel Manufacturing**: Producing TMT rebars, structural steel, and specialized alloys for construction.
- **Heavy Machinery**: Assembling earth-moving equipment, cranes, and custom industrial machinery.
- **Pre-cast Concrete Elements**: Manufacturing ready-to-install concrete segments for rapid infrastructure development.

## Key Terminology
- **MT (Metric Tonnes)**: Standard unit of measurement for bulk raw materials like steel and cement.
- **GRN (Goods Receipt Note)**: An internal document produced after inspecting deliveries for proof of receipt.
- **OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)**: A measure of manufacturing productivity.

## Standard Operating Procedures
- Quality control tests must be documented for every batch of structural steel produced.
- Predictive maintenance schedules are strictly enforced to minimize machine downtime.
- Inventory levels for critical raw materials must maintain a minimum 15-day buffer.
