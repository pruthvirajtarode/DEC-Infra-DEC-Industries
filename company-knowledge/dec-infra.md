# DEC Infra Context

## Overview
DEC Infra is the flagship infrastructure division of the DEC Group, specializing in large-scale public and private construction projects across the country.

## Core Operations
- **Metro and Rail Projects**: Executing complex urban transit systems, including elevated corridors and underground tunnels.
- **Highway Development**: Engineering and constructing national highways and expressways.
- **Commercial Real Estate**: Developing premium corporate parks and commercial centers.

## Key Terminology
- **BOQ (Bill of Quantities)**: A document used in tendering in which materials, parts, and labor are itemized.
- **RA Bill (Running Account Bill)**: Interim payments made to contractors during the progress of a construction project.
- **DPR (Daily Progress Report)**: A daily log of work done, labor employed, and materials consumed at a site.

## Standard Operating Procedures
- Tenders require rigorous risk assessment before submission.
- Procurement must obtain a minimum of three vendor quotes for materials exceeding ₹5 Lakhs.
- Safety audits must be conducted weekly at all active sites.
